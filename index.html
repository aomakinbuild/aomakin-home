About the Service
🏠 What is AO Makin Home?
AO Makin Home is a monthly home maintenance subscription for apartments and villas in Riyadh.

For SAR 100–150/month, you get:
• A preventive visit — routine checks before problems grow
• An emergency visit — fast response when something goes wrong
• Coverage for plumbing, HVAC, electrical, painting & general repair

No call-out fees. No surprise bills. Just a home that works.
📍 Pilot Area
We are launching in Al-Malga & Al-Yasmeen, Riyadh.

Early subscribers in the pilot area receive priority scheduling and a chance to shape the service with their feedback.

Open to both Saudi nationals and expat residents.
